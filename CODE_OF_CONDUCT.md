# Nexvyn UI Community Guidelines

Nexvyn UI is built in public. People arrive with different backgrounds, abilities, tastes, and levels of experience, but everyone should be able to participate without being dismissed, intimidated, or harassed.

These guidelines apply anywhere people take part in the project: issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, community channels, and public conversations made on behalf of Nexvyn UI.

## How we work together

Good collaboration is direct, patient, and specific. In this community, we expect people to:

- Discuss the work without attacking the person behind it
- Explain disagreements with enough context for others to respond
- Treat questions as genuine, including questions from first-time contributors
- Give feedback that someone can act on
- Accept that design and engineering decisions may involve tradeoffs
- Correct mistakes without hiding them or blaming others
- Respect a clear request to end an unproductive interaction

Strong opinions are welcome. Contempt is not. A review can reject an implementation without belittling its author, and a contributor can challenge a decision without turning the discussion into a personal conflict.

## Review and contribution etiquette

Code review is part of the product, not a test of someone's worth or seniority.

When reviewing work:

- Point to the behavior, requirement, or code that needs attention
- Separate required changes from optional preferences
- Avoid piling on after another reviewer has already made the point
- Do not use review access to intimidate, embarrass, or repeatedly block someone without a technical reason

When receiving feedback:

- Ask for clarification when a request is unclear
- Respond to the substance of the review
- Assume good faith unless the pattern of behavior gives a reason not to
- Step away when a conversation is becoming heated

Maintainers may close discussions that repeat settled arguments without adding new technical information.

## What is not acceptable

Nexvyn UI does not tolerate:

- Harassment, threats, stalking, or sustained unwanted contact
- Discrimination or degrading remarks based on identity, background, health, disability, appearance, experience, or personal circumstances
- Sexualized language, imagery, attention, or advances
- Insults, trolling, deliberate humiliation, or personal and political attacks directed at another participant
- Publishing or threatening to publish private information without permission
- Impersonation, deceptive attribution, or deliberate misrepresentation of another person's work
- Retaliation against someone who raises a concern or participates in a moderation review
- Disruptive behavior that continues after a maintainer asks for it to stop

This list is not exhaustive. Maintainers may act on conduct that undermines a safe, professional, and productive community even when the exact behavior is not listed above.

## Inclusion

Participation must remain open and respectful regardless of age, body size, caste, color, disability, education, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sex characteristics, sexual identity or orientation, or socioeconomic status.

People do not need to disclose personal information or defend their identity to receive respectful treatment.

## Reporting a concern

Report conduct concerns through the [Nexvyn UI issue form](https://github.com/Nexvyn/Nexvyn-ui/issues/new).

GitHub issues are public. Do not post addresses, private messages, personal contact details, access tokens, or other sensitive material. A short report can identify the affected community space, link to public evidence, and ask a maintainer to arrange the next step.

A useful report includes:

- Where and when the incident occurred
- A factual description of what happened
- Links or screenshots when they can be shared safely
- Whether the behavior is ongoing
- Any immediate safety or access concern maintainers should address

Knowingly false reports and retaliation are themselves violations. A report made in good faith will not be penalized merely because the available evidence is inconclusive.

## How maintainers respond

Maintainers will review the available context before acting. Whenever possible, a maintainer who is directly involved in the incident will not make the final moderation decision alone.

The response will depend on severity, repetition, impact, and whether the person takes responsibility. Possible actions include:

- Asking for a comment or message to be edited
- Giving a private or public warning
- Locking or closing a discussion
- Reverting or rejecting a contribution
- Restricting participation for a defined period
- Removing repository or community access permanently

Severe threats, harassment, doxxing, or credible safety risks may result in immediate removal without a prior warning. Lower-impact mistakes will usually begin with clarification or correction.

Maintainers will share moderation details only with people who need them. Public explanations may be limited to protect privacy and prevent further harm.

## Representation

These guidelines also apply when someone presents themselves as an official Nexvyn UI representative, uses a project-controlled account, or speaks at an event on the project's behalf. Personal activity outside that context is not governed by this document unless it creates a direct and credible safety issue within the community.

## Changes to these guidelines

The project may revise this document as the community grows. Material changes should be made through the repository so contributors can review the history and discuss the policy openly.
